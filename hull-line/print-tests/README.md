# The print that decides

Four prints of the same block, same filament, same session. Copy one file over
`../com.github.dzwiedziu-nkg.hull-line/settings.lua`, slice the project it names, print, and
look at the wall at the height where the deck starts.

| # | file | project to slice | question it answers |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `1-reference.lua` | `prusa_benchy_hullline_test_block4.3mf` | is there a hull line on this printer at all, and is the wall clean? |
| 2 | `2-order-as-it-was.lua` | the same | the order half as first printed — this is the one that came out faintly marked |
| 3 | `3-order-everywhere.lua` | the same | the same order with no run boundaries: clean here and marked in 2 means the *switch* marks the wall, not the order |
| 4 | `4-prusas-own.lua` | `prusa_benchy_hullline_test_block4_deck.3mf` | Prusa's own experiment: deck fill, hull wall, hull fill, with the wall back in the middle of the layer |

**Print 1 and 4 at least.** All four keep `flow_ratio = 1.0`, so the flow half is out of the way
and the only variable is the order.

The deck project for print 4 is generated by `tools/mkdeckmodifier.py` in the fork's workspace;
in the GUI the same thing is right-click → *Add modifier → Box*, placed over the deck and given
`Perimeters = 0`. It costs +2.3 % filament on this block, which is what walling the new region
boundary costs, and that price is the same whether or not the plugin is installed.

`tools/hullline_prints.sh <output dir>` in the workspace slices all four in one go, if you would
rather have the G-code than edit settings by hand.

## What the four slices come out as

Sliced against the owner's own project and profile — a check that the settings did what they say
before anything is printed:

| # | reordered | filament | where in the layer the wall is laid, layers 20-26 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | — | 650.74 mm | 20 13 20 20 20 19 21 % |
| 2 | 10 of 40 | 650.74 mm | 20 **90** 85 85 85 86 20 % |
| 3 | 40 of 40 | 650.74 mm | 85 90 85 85 85 86 75 % |
| 4 | 10 of 40 | 665.75 mm | 20 **51** 37 37 37 43 21 % |

Prints 2 and 3 move not one milligram of material — they only move the wall. Print 4's +15.01 mm
is the modifier's region boundary being walled, and is there with or without the plugin.

What the answers mean is in the main [README](../README.md#the-print-that-decides).
